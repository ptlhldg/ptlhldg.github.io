Who We Are

A Legacy Built on
Disciplined Capital

Patel Holdings is a private multigenerational family firm diversifying wealth across investment funds, operating businesses, real estate, and global ventures. Our approach centers on capital preservation and long-term compounding.

Investment Philosophy

We focus on diversified asset allocation across traditional and alternative investments, with particular emphasis on real estate, hospitality, and emerging markets. Every decision is made through a generational lens — not a quarterly one.

Our Vision

To create sustainable wealth while upholding family values and contributing to economic growth across the communities and economies we serve worldwide.
