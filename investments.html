Our Investment Divisions

Patel Holdings LLC

A private, multigenerational family firm committed to long-term capital growth and preservation. Our diversified investment platform spans across in-house funds, direct ownership of businesses, real estate development, and international ventures.

Growing Family Investment Fund

Our core in-house managed equity fund, broadly diversified across equities, fixed income, real assets, and cash equivalents. Structured for disciplined growth and intergenerational wealth transfer.

Operating Businesses

We maintain active ownership in a range of operating businesses that generate stable income, including retail, hospitality, and entertainment assets across North America. Our portfolio is strategically balanced by geography and sector.

Real Estate Holdings

Our real estate division oversees development and ownership of residential, commercial, and hospitality projects. Development is handled through a sister entity, with long-term ownership retained by Patel Holdings.

Bright Future Investment Fund

A flexible, next-generation-focused fund that supports innovation, liquidity, and entrepreneurship. Designed to empower younger family members through strategic exposure across multiple asset classes.

International Holdings

We maintain a global investment footprint with land, real estate, and business holdings in over 8 countries, including Canada, UK, India, Australia, France, and South Africa. These holdings support diversification and global market access.
