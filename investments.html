Our Investment Divisions

Patel Holdings LLC

Patel Holdings is a private, multigenerational family firm committed to long-term capital growth and preservation. Our diversified investment platform spans across a mix of managed funds, operating companies, real estate, and international ventures.

Growing Family Investment Fund

Our flagship investment fund, focused on diversified, long-term growth. The fund is managed in-house and allocated across public markets, real assets, and fixed income vehicles to support capital expansion across generations.

Operating Businesses

We maintain direct ownership of a portfolio of operating businesses across various sectors. These companies provide consistent cash flow and are strategically selected for geographic and industry diversification.

Real Estate Holdings

Our real estate division encompasses both development and ownership of residential, commercial, and hospitality assets. This portfolio supports both income generation and long-term asset value preservation.

Bright Future Investment Fund

A next-generation fund designed to maintain liquidity, encourage innovation, and support entrepreneurial ventures. Managed by Patel Holdings, this fund seeks to cultivate growth opportunities aligned with the family’s evolving values.

International Holdings

Global exposure is a core part of our strategy. We hold a presence across multiple countries through a blend of private investments, land development, and international partnerships.
