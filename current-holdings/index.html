Operating Businesses
$97,501,513 · 28.01% of total portfolio

Our operating businesses represent the largest single pillar of Patel Holdings, providing stable cash flow and active value creation across multiple industries and geographies. Each business is managed with a long-term ownership mindset, minimizing unnecessary turnover and maximizing compounding returns.

Hospitality & Lodging

A portfolio of branded and independent motels across the US and Canada, focused on high-traffic corridors and interstate locations. Properties operate under both franchise and proprietary banners.

Fuel & Convenience

Gas station and convenience store operations concentrated in the Northeast and Southeast US, with several locations featuring branded quick-service restaurant (QSR) components integrated on-site.

Retail Beverage & Liquor

Liquor store holdings across New York and New Jersey, including recent acquisitions in the NY–NJ metro area completed in October 2025, expanding shelf footprint and licensing positions in high-density markets.

Franchise Chains & Entertainment

Franchise agreements and entertainment venue interests diversified across regions, providing additional revenue streams and geographic risk diversification beyond core hospitality assets.

Real Estate Holdings
$67,637,425 · 19.43% of total portfolio

Real estate holdings are focused on wealth preservation and income generation through tangible, appreciating assets in high-performing markets. The portfolio spans residential, multifamily, commercial, and hospitality-adjacent properties developed in partnership with our affiliate real estate firm.

Residential & Multifamily

Single-family and multifamily residential assets concentrated in growth markets across the US, selected for strong rental demand, low vacancy rates, and long-term appreciation potential. Properties are managed third-party for operational efficiency.

Commercial Real Estate

Retail strip centers, mixed-use properties, and light industrial assets generating stable, NNN-anchored cash flows. Focused on markets with strong employment bases and limited new supply.

Hospitality Properties

Hotel and extended-stay assets that complement the operating businesses division, allowing vertical integration of property ownership and operational management under the Patel Holdings umbrella.

International Holdings
$59,627,583 · 17.13% of total portfolio

International holdings are maintained for global diversification and to capture emerging market opportunities across multiple geographies and currencies. This allocation supports the family's long-term vision for global impact and expands exposure to different economies beyond the North American core.

Canada

Land holdings and real estate investments in key Canadian metropolitan areas, supplementing the North American operating business footprint and providing currency and asset diversification.

United Kingdom

Private business interests in London and surrounding regions, including the registered office at 50 Jermyn St, St. James's, London. Focused on service and financial-adjacent business ventures.

India

Operating ventures and land holdings in Gujarat, managed through local partners. Includes the Anand operations center in Vithal Udyognagar GIDC, with exposure to India's growing domestic economy.

France & Australia

Land and property holdings across Europe and the Asia-Pacific region, held for long-term appreciation and strategic optionality within the global portfolio.
